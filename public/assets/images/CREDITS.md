# Photographs

Every photograph in this directory is from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and
is used under the **[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license)**: free for
commercial and non-commercial use, no permission needed, no attribution
required.

Attribution is not required, but it is recorded here anyway. Provenance that
lives only in someone's memory is provenance the next person has to re-establish
from scratch — and a shop cannot answer "where did this picture come from?" with
a shrug.

Every image was checked to be a real photograph rather than a 3D render. A large
share of Unsplash's "minimalist furniture" results are CGI, and a rendered sofa
on a page that promises real measurements is the same lie as an invented
dimension.

## How these were prepared

Downloaded at 2400px from `images.unsplash.com`, then cropped to the aspect the
layout actually draws — 4:3 for product cards and the product page, 4:5 for the
marketing diptych — and saved as JPEG at quality 88.

Cropping here rather than leaving it to `object-fit: cover` means the framing is
a decision someone made and looked at. Two of these needed it badly: the sofa is
a room photograph cropped down onto the sofa, and without that it reads as a
living room rather than as a piece for sale.

The JPEGs are only the fallback. AVIF and WebP derivatives at six widths are
generated by `php artisan images:derive` and are what browsers actually fetch.

`product-placeholder.jpg` is not a photograph. It is a flat `--color-paper-3`
field with a hairline cross, drawn for the case where a product has no image of
its own. It deliberately does not look like a photograph that failed to load,
and it deliberately is not a picture of some other product — which is what the
fallback used to be.

## Replacing one

1. Drop the new file in at the aspect its slot uses (see the table in
   `ProductImageFactory::BY_TYPE` for which slot a file serves).
2. Run `php artisan images:derive --force` to rebuild its derivatives.
3. Add the photographer and source to the table above.
