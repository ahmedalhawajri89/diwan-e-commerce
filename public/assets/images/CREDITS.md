# Photographs

Every photograph in this directory is from [Unsplash](https://unsplash.com) and
is used under the **[Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license)**: free for
commercial and non-commercial use, no permission needed, no attribution
required. None is an Unsplash+ (paid) image: each was downloaded through the
free download endpoint, which redirects to `images.unsplash.com`.

Attribution is not required, but it is recorded here anyway. Provenance that
lives only in someone's memory is provenance the next person has to re-establish
from scratch, and a shop cannot answer "where did this picture come from?" with
a shrug.

Every image was looked at before it was accepted and checked to be a real
photograph rather than a 3D render. A large share of Unsplash's "minimalist
furniture" results are CGI, and a rendered sofa on a page that promises real
measurements is the same lie as an invented dimension. Renders that were
rejected on that ground include every result from interior-visualisation
studios.

## How these were prepared

Downloaded at 2600px or more, then cropped by hand, around a chosen focal
point, to the aspect the layout actually draws:

| Use | Aspect | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product cards and product pages (`product-*`) | 4:5 | 1600 × 2000 |
| The home page photograph (`hero-living-room`) | 16:9 | 2560 × 1440 |
| The workshop band (`workshop`) | 16:9 | 2400 × 1350 |
| The sign-in pages (`auth-panel`) | 4:5 | 1600 × 2000 |

Nothing was upscaled: every crop was taken from a source at least as large as
the file it became. Saved as progressive JPEG at quality 86 with the camera
metadata removed.

Cropping here rather than leaving it all to `object-fit: cover` means the
framing is a decision someone made and looked at.

The JPEGs are only the fallback. AVIF and WebP derivatives are generated by
`php artisan images:derive` and are what browsers actually fetch.

## Which product gets which photograph

There are three photographs of each kind of piece. `ProductImageFactory`
deals them out in turn among products of the same kind, so two armchairs do
not wear the same picture until all three have been used. An existing
catalogue is brought in line with `php artisan db:seed --class=StockImageSeeder`,
which never touches a photograph an admin uploaded.

`product-placeholder.jpg` is not a photograph. It is a flat `--color-paper-3`

and it deliberately is not a picture of some other product.

## Replacing one

1. Crop the new file to the aspect and size of its slot (table above).
2. Run `php artisan images:derive --force` to rebuild its derivatives.
3. Record the photographer and source in the table above.
